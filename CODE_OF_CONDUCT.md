# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for the people who rely on these tools

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Submitting content designed to compromise users of these plugins (for example, a skill or script that exfiltrates data)

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer through a GitHub issue marked "conduct" or through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
